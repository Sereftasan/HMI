Robot X – axis information

CAUTION A servo axis holds position with the brake and the drive enabled. Before working inside its travel, stop the machine, switch off and lock out the supply, and support the carriage mechanically. Releasing the brake with the load unsupported lets a vertical axis drop.
POINT This sheet is about the axis itself: its drive, its limits and its homing. For a drive alarm, open the servo drive manual from the alarm code shown on the Axis Settings page – it is written for that code.

What it moves

Robot X carries the transfer arm along the machine, from the entry conveyor to the exit station. Ball screw driven through a toothed belt, with the reference (home) sensor at the entry end.

Homing

The axis homes towards the entry end at setup speed until the reference sensor switches, then creeps back to the encoder's Z pulse. Home after any of these: a drive battery change, a motor or encoder change, a belt change, or any alarm that mentions position loss.

StepWhat to do
1Clear the travel: no profile in the transfer station, no tooling in the path.
2Select setup mode and put the machine in manual.
3Jog the axis until it is clear of the reference sensor, towards the exit end.
4Start the homing cycle and let it finish without interruption.
5Check the position reading against the scale mark at the entry end: it must read 0.00 ± 0.05 mm.

TaskIntervalHow
Ball screw lubricationEvery 500 running hoursTwo strokes of grease at the nipple, with the axis at mid travel.
Belt tensionEvery 6 monthsAbout 3 mm deflection under light thumb pressure at mid span. Re-home after re-tensioning.
Backlash checkEvery 6 monthsApproach a mark from both directions at setup speed and compare the readings. Above 0.15 mm, ask for support.
Drive batteryEvery 2 yearsReplace with the drive powered, or the absolute position is lost and the axis must be homed.

After a servo alarm

Note the alarm code from the Axis Settings page before resetting it: the drive keeps only the most recent ones. Reset, then jog the axis both ways at setup speed and watch for noise or jerking movement. If the same code returns, leave the machine stopped and pass the code to maintenance – repeated resets of an overload or overheat alarm damage the motor.

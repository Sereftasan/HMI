Robot Top Clamping - unit information

CAUTION Before any manual check on this unit, isolate and lock out the electrical and pneumatic supplies and release the stored pressure. The clamp can move suddenly when air is restored. Never bypass the guard door interlock to reach it.
POINT This sheet describes what the unit IS and where it is. For a movement failure, open the troubleshooting help from the alarm instead: it is written for the fault, not for the part.

What it does

The top clamp holds the profile against the robot's reference face while the robot transfers it to the exit conveyor. It clamps before the transfer starts and releases only after the profile is supported downstream. A single-acting stroke of about 25 mm, driven by a 5/2 double solenoid valve, with one magnetic sensor confirming the clamped end position.

Fig. 1 - Robot top clamp: valve, cylinder and the clamped-end sensor

Where it is

On the exit side of the machining centre, on the upper cross beam above the robot transfer station. The valve sits on the exit-side valve island, positions 7 and 8; the sensor cable runs to terminal box TB-4. Setting and maintenance

TaskIntervalHow
Clamping forceOn commissioning and after any valve change Set the supply to 6 bar and the exhaust throttles so the stroke takes about 0.4 s. The clamp must hold the profile without marking it.
Sensor positionAfter any cylinder or sensor change Clamp on an actual profile, then slide the sensor until it switches, and move it 1 mm further in. Check that it stays on with the thinnest and the thickest profile in production.
Clamp padMonthlyCheck for wear and for material build-up; replace if the surface is polished smooth.
Leak checkMonthlyWith the clamp held, listen at the valve exhaust. Continuous blow-by means worn seals.

After replacing a part

Run the movement at setup speed from the HMI's Manual Movement page, both directions, at least three times, and confirm the clamped sensor lights on the left button every time. Then run one automatic cycle with an operator watching before releasing the machine to production.

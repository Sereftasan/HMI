Does the axis home towards its sensor?

  • {{>Look}} The sensor is on {{>RefSide}}. Watch a homing from a safe place: the axis must move towards that side{{#Moved}} – this time it moved{{>Travel}}{{/Moved}}{{#AwayFromRef}}, away from it: this check first{{/AwayFromRef}}.
  • {{>Hand}} Moving away from the sensor: the module's homing direction (Pr.44) does not match the sensor's side, or the motor's direction was changed (the amplifier's PA14). An authorized person with GX Works3 checks it.
  • {{>Look}} Started on the far side of the sensor, the axis may have to pass it first: start the homing from the middle of the travel to see the difference.

Is the axis free to move?

    {{#Stuck}}
  • {{>Look}} It moved{{>Travel}} and then stood still for {{StuckSeconds}} s before the code came: it was pushing against something.
    • {{/Stuck}}
  • {{>Hand}} Lock out the machine first. Then look along the whole travel{{>Where}}: something in its way (a profile piece, a clamp, a tool, another unit), marks of a crash, a bent part.
  • {{>Look}} The drive: the belt or rack and its pinion, the ball screw and its nut, the coupling to the motor (loose or slipping), the guides and carriages (dry, damaged, full of chips).
  • {{>Hand}} Where it is safe and the axis is not vertical: push it by hand with the power off – it should move evenly, without a hard spot.
  • {{>Ear}} Back in operation: move it slowly over its travel and listen; watch its load (the current bar on the Axis page) – a load that rises at one spot shows where it drags.

Does the holding brake open?

  • {{>Ear}} When the servo turns on, the brake must click open; listen at the motor.
  • {{>Measure}} No click: measure the brake's 24 V at the motor's brake connector while the servo is on (the brake relay in the cabinet switches it).
  • {{>Hand}} A vertical axis falls when its brake opens without the servo holding it: support the load before anything is done to the brake.
  • {{>Hand}} A brake that drags warms the motor's end: feel it after a few movements.

  • {{>Look}} A limit beyond the end lets the axis run into it. Since the last homing the reference may have moved (a slipped coupling or belt): then every position is off by the same amount.

Speed and acceleration for its load?

  • {{>Look}} Watch its load (the current bar on the Axis page) during the movement: near or above 100 % on every start or stop means the acceleration or deceleration is too high for the load.
  • {{>Hand}} Lower the speed (the speed override) and try again: no alarm at a lower speed confirms it. The settings themselves are for an authorized person.

The limit switch

  • {{>Look}} Is the axis standing on its limit switch{{>Where}}? Move it back by hand, the other way, slowly.
  • {{>Look}} Not at the end: the switch or its wiring is faulty (a broken wire reads as a reached limit) – see the Inputs / Outputs page.

Home the axis

  • {{>Run}} Reset the alarm and home the axis (its Ref box on the Axis page, or the machine's homing), then start again.

The module's homing settings

  • {{>Hand}} The homing method (Pr.43), direction (Pr.44), speeds (Pr.46, Pr.47) and the zero signal settings are checked in GX Works3 by an authorized person; the code's entry of the manual names the setting.

The amplifier's battery

  • {{>Look}} The amplifier's display shows a battery warning (9F) when it is low. Replace it with the control power ON, or the position is lost again.
  • {{>Run}} Then home the axis.

The encoder cable

  • {{>Hand}} With the power off: the encoder connector at the motor and at the amplifier (CN2) – seated and locked, no bent pins, no oil inside.
  • {{>Look}} The cable along the cable chain: crushed or worn spots; it must not run tight beside motor or power cables. Its shield on the connector's housing.

The supply and the ready signals

  • {{>Measure}} The amplifier's control circuit supply (L11 / L21) and main circuit supply; the emergency stop chain closed.
  • {{>Look}} The PLC in RUN (PLC READY), the servo ready on the Axis Monitor.
